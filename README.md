🌈 Welcome to PLAYORA

PLAYORA Toys is a playful ecommerce storefront concept created around one simple idea:

Great toys should spark imagination before the box is even opened.

The store combines the excitement of a children's toy shop with the clarity and professionalism adults expect when choosing products for children.

PLAYORA is designed for:

Parents

Grandparents

Families

Gift shoppers

Teachers

Collectors

Children exploring with an adult

Anyone looking for a memorable play experience

The goal is to make discovering a toy feel fun, while keeping the shopping experience organized, clear, and comfortable.

🚀 The PLAYORA Experience

PLAYORA is built as more than a simple product catalog.

It can guide shoppers from:

Discover → Explore → Compare → Choose → Gift → Play

The experience can combine colorful merchandising, realistic product photography, age-oriented discovery, themed collections, gift ideas, educational categories, and straightforward customer support.

📸 Photography Direction

Product imagery should be realistic and easy to understand.

Recommended visual approach:

Clear product photography

Bright, clean backgrounds

Realistic proportions

Good visibility of the full product

Multiple angles when useful

Lifestyle imagery for selected products

Consistent framing

No unrelated UI elements inside product photos

No fake badges or buttons embedded into product photography

Product images should always correspond to the actual item being sold.
